This document describes the use of encryption within the Eigen Pocket iOS
application, in support of the App Encryption Documentation requirement
for distribution on the App Store, including in France.

1. SUMMARY OF ENCRYPTION USE

Eigen Pocket uses encryption solely to protect data in transit between the
application and the EigenVertex backend over the public internet. This is
implemented exclusively through TLS (HTTPS), using Apple's standard
networking APIs (URLSession / the iOS Network framework) and the
encryption built into the iOS operating system. The app also stores
authentication tokens using the iOS Keychain, whose encryption is
implemented and managed entirely by the operating system.

2. WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO

- The app does not implement, embed, or distribute any proprietary or
  non-standard cryptographic algorithm.
- The app does not implement any cryptographic primitive itself; it relies
  exclusively on the standard, publicly available TLS implementation
  provided by the iOS operating system.
- The app does not use encryption to protect the confidentiality of
  locally stored user content (audio recordings and notes are stored
  unencrypted in the app's local sandboxed storage, protected only by
  standard iOS file-system and app-sandbox protections).
- The app contains no encryption functionality beyond what is required to
  establish a standard HTTPS connection.

3. CLASSIFICATION

The only cryptographic functionality in Eigen Pocket is the use of
standard, publicly available encryption algorithms (TLS/HTTPS) for
securing data in transit, as provided by the operating system. This
qualifies for the "mass market" / standard encryption exemption described
in Category 5, Part 2 of the EAR (15 CFR 740.17(b)(1)), and corresponds to
the equivalent exemption for the declaration of encryption-using products
under French regulations (Article 30 of Law No. 2004-575, implemented via
Decree No. 2007-663), as the app uses only standard, off-the-shelf
encryption for communication security and performs no cryptographic
operations of its own.
